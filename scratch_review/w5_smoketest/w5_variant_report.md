## Method

- **Perimeter time series:** NIFC ArcGIS `WFIGS_Daily_Perimeters_Public` FeatureServer layer 0 — researched live: unlike the `_Current` / `_YearToDate` / `InterAgencyFirePerimeterHistory` layers (one latest/final polygon per fire), the Daily layer keeps every published capture as its own row keyed by `poly_PolygonDateTime` (up to 110 rows per IRWIN id measured). Public domain (NIFC open data), keyless.
- **Observed weather:** Open-Meteo HISTORICAL archive (`archive-api.open-meteo.com`, ERA5-family reanalysis) hourly at the fire centroid, wind in m/s, UTC. **Licensing:** free tier is NON-COMMERCIAL and data is CC-BY-4.0 — this harness is an offline R&D calibration study; production use requires the paid tier or self-hosting (same posture as `wgresponse.weather`).
- **Terrain/fuel:** live LANDFIRE LFPS tiles (LF2024_FBFM40 + LF2020 slope/aspect/elev) through the engine's own `terrain.LfpsTerrain`, disk-cached. LF2024 fuels predate the replayed fires (pre-fire landscape — correct for hindcasting).
- **Replay:** for each consecutive snapshot pair (T0, T1): seed `spread.ensemble_arrival` with the T0 rings via the production perimeter-interior path (rings subsampled to <= 200 vertices, `perimeter_at_s = t0 = T0`, no exterior seeds), shipped default `ResponseConfig` (ensemble_n=6, wind_perturb_frac=0.25, moisture_perturb_frac=0.2, dir_perturb_deg=30.0, spotting=OFF), observed weather as the forecast.
- **Scoring under censoring (CORRECTED 2026-08-31 — tri-model review, fix-program item 5; full definitions in docs/calibration/SCORECARD.md):** a perimeter pair only brackets each newly-burned cell's true burn time inside (T0, T1]. `late_miss` = p10 > T1 (band entirely after the window — engine definitively LATE; the DANGEROUS, missed-alert polarity) is the honest statistic. `covered_certain`, `covered_possible`, and `early_miss` are DEPRECATED: because the engine seeds at T0, no modeled arrival can precede T0, so p90 >= T0 ALWAYS holds. That makes `early_miss` (p90 < T0) STRUCTURALLY IMPOSSIBLE (~0 in every pool ever scored — a tautology of the replay design, not evidence the ensemble is well-centered) and collapses `covered_possible` (band intersects [T0,T1]) EXACTLY onto `p10 <= T1` — a ONE-SIDED statistic reported honestly below as `p10_adequacy`. `covered_certain` needs `p10 <= T0`, which is likewise ~0 by the same construction. All three still appear, clearly labelled, in a deprecated appendix table for schema continuity — never as calibration evidence. The informative statistics are p10_adequacy, late_miss, head-ratio and area-ratio at p10/p50/p90/nominal, the PIT histogram (approximated at the window midpoint, summarized by extreme-bin mass + a flatness statistic), the null-model skill, window-length bins, and incident concentration. Cells whose LANDFIRE code is non-burnable (urban etc.) can never receive a modeled arrival — they are counted and reported, never silently dropped.
- **Null-model baseline:** an isotropic (Euclidean nearest-first) dilation of the engine-T0 interior, grown to the SAME cell count as the engine's own p10 new-area budget (`squared_edt` / `null_model_covered` in tools/hindcast_lib.py, ported from the tri-model review's independent verification script `scratch_review/rv2sci_a2_null.py`) — a physics-free 'just grow a circle' control. `skill` = p10_adequacy / null_adequacy at that equal area; skill > 1x means the physics beats the circle.
- **Head run:** farthest newly-burned boundary cell from the T0 boundary (straight-line, metric grid), observed vs the modeled p50/p10 burned region at T1. Boundary sets are strided to caps, so both numbers are conservative floors (same bias both sides of the ratio).

### Reading these numbers (interpretation caveats)

- **Suppression is unmodeled.** These fires were actively fought; on contained flanks and late-stage pairs the engine over-runs the observed growth (head/area ratios well above 1, PIT mass at the top rank read 'engine early'). That residual is firefighting, NOT evidence to slow the engine down — and it is the fail-safe direction for an alerting product.
- **The safety-relevant evidence is the explosive-run pairs** (first hours/day of each fire, low head-run ratios, high late miss): there the engine is definitively LATE — no member reaches cells that actually burned inside the window. Those are the pairs a missed alert would have come from.
- **ERA5 reanalysis wind is smooth.** Hourly 10-m reanalysis at the fire centroid underestimates canyon/gap gusts and local fire-weather extremes (max sustained 10-m wind across every cached series here: 5.7 m/s), so part of the late bias may be input-side, not physics-side. A HRRR-analysis or RAWS-station replay is the follow-up that would split the two.

## RECOMMENDATION

- P10 pessimism (F8 target >= 90% of observed arrivals at/after p10): best measurable bound 99% (p10 <= T1; the sufficient bound p10 <= T0 is STRUCTURALLY ~0 in this replay — the observation window opens at the seed time and p10 can never precede the seed, so it carries no information) on 60062 cells / 14 pairs.
- The necessary bound (99%) clears the bar; the censored windows cannot prove the sufficient side. Needed: shorter-dt pairs (FIRIS intra-day perimeters) before changing any perturbation knob.
- Tubbs anchor (spotting OFF): engine/observed head ratio 0.46x (GR2) to 0.89x (SH5) vs the review's 0.47x point-ROS measurement — the systematic-slow finding stands. A grass-ROS multiplier (ELMFIRE-style ADJ) is DIRECTIONALLY supported by the anchors but NOT yet by enough hindcast coverage evidence to pick a number; do not ship one from this document alone.

## DO NOT SHIP A FUDGE

No ROS multiplier, perturbation widening, or quantile remap may ship from
this document alone. The firerisk honest-numbers discipline applies:

- The replay sample here is small (a handful of incidents, interval-censored
  observation windows, one fire season, one state) and the landmark anchors
  are two hand-parameterized scenarios. That is EVIDENCE OF DIRECTION, not a
  calibration fit.
- Any proposed `ResponseConfig` change must first be re-run through this
  harness on an EXPANDED incident set (>= 10 incidents, multiple fuel
  regimes, both wind-driven and terrain-driven fires) and show coverage
  moving toward the target WITHOUT wrecking the early-miss side (a p10 that
  is pessimistic-everywhere is a false-alarm machine that erodes crew trust
  and burns dispatch capacity).
- A grass-ROS ADJ multiplier additionally needs the spotting decision made
  first (F3: spotting is the mechanism that made Camp/Tubbs beat every
  no-spotting model) — tuning surface ROS to absorb missing spotting physics
  bakes ember transport into grass parameters where it does not belong.
- Per SAFETY SEMANTICS, any change lands as config + regression pins, with
  before/after coverage tables appended here, and byte-identity pins
  re-baselined explicitly — never silently.
