# Eagle River valley review, iteration 0: fire propagation and fire-behaviour realism (lens `propagation`)

Method: each run's nominal field re-solved with `spread.compute_arrival` on the harness's `build()` session (reproduces the baseline acres and head columns exactly for all 27; 0.1-1 s a 12-h solve): about 15 variant matrices over the 27 distinct fires, plus one full 12-h harness matrix with the live posture. Probes `p1`-`p26`, maps, `fieldlib.py`: `out/eagle_valley/probes/propagation/`. External: 13 summers of KEGE hourly observations, the CDOT RWIS stations in the valley (Iowa Mesonet), fire analogs by web search. "Homes reached" = the simulator's `eta_at` law (own cell, else earliest burnable cell within 200 m), nominal fire, 12 h; 27 fires x 40 homes = 1,080 home-runs. `wgresponse/` and `tests/` untouched.

## Verdict

Plausible in level, not in timing, and the timing errors that matter to crews mostly run anti-safe (the simulated fire later than a real one). The fastest hour is a median 7.8 % of that hour's wind (1.6-14.5 %), inside the 10 % +-50 % benchmark, and 6-h areas at "high" (1,633 / 3,979 / 5,624 acres) are 1.3-4.3x Grizzly Creek's ~1,300 acres by nightfall. But crown fire and spotting carry the speed (table), and the crews' deadline field, the P10, is only 0.90x the nominal arrival where the live watcher's posture gives 0.46x (that posture held 99.8 % of newly burned ground on the 11-fire hindcast). The P10 width, the dawn start and the frozen edge law matter more to crews than any speed figure.

## Findings (crew meaning in italics)

**F1 Crown fire is a switch set by canopy base height.** 84-89 % of burned crown-capable cells (Vail / Beaver Creek / corridor, canonical high) have LANDFIRE CBH < 1 m; TU5 under conifer burned as active crown in 2,546 of 2,548 Vail cells. CBH at 2 m: Vail canonical high 14,537 -> 2,570 acres and 7 homes -> 0; Beaver Creek canonical high first home 3.4 -> 5.6 h. The nominal sits at the fast end and the ensemble has no CBH dimension. Two omissions pull the other way: no slope term in the crown rate, and unsheltered surface wind under canopy (midflame x0.65 on TU/TL/SH4: totals +2 %, single fires +-50 %, first home +-2-3 h). Sylvan (same KEGE class: 21 mph gusting 30, 13 % RH) burned ~1,424 acres in its first day; the simulated 12-h areas are >= 10x that (no suppression; F3). *Arrival at a given home is "crown or not", +-2-5 h.*

**F2 The stall-then-jump runs are two different things.** eagle_vail|northwest|moderate (10 acres in 12 h) is an artifact of the frozen departure-hour edge law: the first edge is priced at the 07:13 weather (SH2 head ~7 m/h at 3 mph and 64 % RH, against 251 m/h at 14:00), so the second cell burns at +36.7 h (piecewise: +5.1 h; 96-h area 206k vs 305k acres). beaver_creek|northwest|moderate (2 acres for 6 h) is mostly physical dawn calm (+6.1 h vs +5.8 h piecewise). On the other moderate fires the law moves area -3 to +58 % and the first home 0.2-1.4 h earlier (4 of 9); high/extreme (constant weather) are unmoved. Field time x2.9 at 36 h, x2.6-4.5 at 96 h; skipping fast edges gives the same field and no saving (tested). *Late-biased, so anti-safe, in the live engine too.*

**F3 Weather is one KEGE dial from dawn.** (a) Every run starts 07:13 local. High/extreme hold afternoon values from ignition, so start hour does not matter to their first 6 h; moderate fires lit at 13:13 (Sylvan 15:15, Grizzly 13:30 daylight time) reach the first home a median 2.95 h sooner (five of six runs, -1.6 to -6.1 h). (b) KEGE afternoon wind is 251-258 deg (61-73 % in 200-290), so 240 deg is right for events, but 22:00-07:00 flow is down-valley from ~100 deg (72-82 % from 40-130) and the simulator never reverses. (c) On KEGE >= 18 mph afternoons Dowd Junction reads 0.57x (12 mph) from 284 deg, Wolcott 0.47x from 291 deg, Vail Pass 0.35x; the simulator applies KEGE speed and 240 deg at Vail and Avon. At 0.6x wind canonical high reaches 1 / 2 / 3 homes against 7 / 12 / 6 (Vail / Beaver Creek / corridor). (d) The half-cosine cycle is 2-3x observed at 10-11:00 (10.0 vs 3.3 mph) and ~2x after 19:00 (14.4 vs 8.7); the observed shape cuts 12-h area 18-48 % in 7 of 9 moderate fires. (e) High/extreme get the event from 07:13 and then the moderate day's own afternoon peak, >= 15 mph for all 12 h where a KEGE high day has ~7: an observed-shape high day lit at 12:13 / 14:13 burns 43 % / 26 % of the simulated 12-h area and Vail canonical reaches 0 homes, not 7. The first 3 h are robust (79-90 %); beyond ~6 h the simulation is an upper envelope. *Moderate rows hand crews a calm morning; the valley floor is probably slower than modelled, slopes and ridges faster.*

**F4 The P10 is narrow.** Ensemble: wind +-12.5 %, moisture +-10 %, direction +-30 deg, `model_error_sd_log` 0 (live: 0.71, plus gust blend 0.5, phi-vector slope, floor gate). P10/nominal arrival at the homes: median 0.90 (10th percentile 0.71) vs 0.46 live (0.37); first-home P10 Vail canonical extreme 5.3 -> 2.0 h. The simulator's P10 arrives later than the live engine's nominal at 84 of the 171 home-runs the latter reaches (35 % by > 30 min; median 0.74 h, max 4.5 h). Full-posture matrix, 27 runs like for like: served 823 -> 760 (high -8 %, extreme -14 %), relocations 26 -> 61, withdrawn 0 -> 4, crews off map 2 -> 14; violations and overruns 0 / 0 both ways; closest-margin median 0.88 -> 0.89 h (truth = nominal, so the benefit cannot show here). *This is the crews' deadline field.*

**F5 The 200 m corridor tile is not a stand-in for the 90 m tiles.** Same weather, ignition on the nearest fine-fuel patch, 16 sites: sd of ln(area ratio) 1.5-1.7; 8 of 16 differ > 2x (0.05-100x), no sign. The same coordinates (Vail canonical origin) are SH4 at 90 m and TL1 at 200 m: 14,537 vs 10 acres. I-70's centreline is 77-78 % non-burnable at 90 m, 54 % at 200 m.

**F6 Embers and the valley floor.** In 7 of the 8 fires checked, 2-13 homes get an ember within 300 m before the front's ETA (0-5 of them never reached by the front in 12 h): median lead 0.45-1.8 h, max 5.5 h. An ember on a developed (non-burnable) cell ignites nothing and gives the home no ETA (`spread.py` teleport edge keeps burnable landings only). Flights reach 2.0-3.3 km (5.5 km, Vail south extreme); cells beyond I-70 burn at +2.8 to +8.1 h, 4-21 % spot-ignited. *The ETA crews plan against trails the embers.*

## Recommendations (ranked by value for crew decisions; SAFETY-CRITICAL marked)

**R1 SAFETY-CRITICAL: arm the live watcher's P10 posture in the simulator.** Change: `simui.SimSession._init_state` sim-defaults block (beside `ensemble_p50_nominal_bound`, ~l.2808): `model_error_sd_log` 0.71, `wind_gust_blend` 0.5, `slope_phi_vector` True, `ros_floor_extinction_gate` True unless in `overrides`; all four exist in `ResponseConfig`, off by default; tests that pin sim numbers pin them off. Effect: P10/nominal 0.90 -> 0.46; first-home P10 1.5-4 h earlier. Risk: served -8 % (-14 % extreme), relocations x2.3, 12 more crews off the map; a middle step, `model_error_sd_log` 0.35, gives 0.69. Measure: `run_matrix.py DIR --cfg '{"model_error_sd_log":0.71,"wind_gust_blend":0.5,"slope_phi_vector":true,"ros_floor_extinction_gate":true}'` then `summarize.py baseline DIR` (done: `prodposture/`); R8's hidden-truth arm is the only place the benefit shows.

**R2 SAFETY-CRITICAL: light the harness's and the one-click fires in the afternoon too.** Change: a `start_local_hour` option on `SimSession` and `POST /api/sim/new` shifting `T0_S` and `_t0_s` (weather is a pure function of absolute time; default None = 07:13, byte-identical); run every set at 07:13 and 13:13. Effect: moderate first home a median 2.95 h sooner. Risk: tests that pin the epoch. Measure: `p5` (add `--start` to `run_matrix.py`).

**R3 SAFETY-CRITICAL: end the frozen departure-hour edge law in the simulator.** Change: arm `edge_weather_piecewise` in `_init_state` (the 5.3 % no-op measurement in `docs/review/city_weather_2026-10-09.md` was high-class on three maps); offset the cost with `ensemble_workers`. Effect: no 36.7-h single-cell stall; moderate first home up to 1.4 h earlier. Risk: field time x2.6-4.5. Measure: runs <= 20 acres at 12 h (now 1 of 27), second-cell time, moderate rows (`p4`, `p21`, `p26b`).

**R4 SAFETY-CRITICAL: ember ETA for developed homes.** Change: record the first ember landing per cell (the teleport edge already computes it, non-burnable cells included) in a side raster behind `cfg.ember_eta_landings` (default off); `spread.eta_at` / `_ember_eroded` take the earlier of the front ETA within `ember_threat_radius_m` and the first landing within it. Effect: F6 (a lower bound: one ember per cell). Risk: more alerts. Measure: `p18`, reached-unprotected.

**R5 Slope term on the crown rate.** Change: `spread._pack_crown` / `_crown_lookup` (~l.1366-1441) hand `rothermel.crown_fire` an open wind = |U10 + slope-equivalent wind| (Canadian FBP slope factor, upslope), ratio capped 2.16, behind `cfg.crown_slope_wind` (default off). Effect: +17 % area, 185 vs 165 homes, 22 % of home ETAs > 1 h earlier (10 % later). Risk: steep-slope runaway (the cap). Measure: `p22` (`spread_slopecrown.py`) and P10 adequacy on the 11-fire hindcast (`scratch_review/crown_fire_hindcast.py`).

**R6 KEGE-observed weather, night reversal, valley transfer.** Change: `weather.DailyCycle` / `simui._city_cycle` take the observed hourly table (onset 10-12, decay 17-20 local; `p17`) and the down-valley direction (~105 deg) outside ~08:30-19:30, behind `cfg.sim_weather_shape` ("halfcos" default); valley floor at ~0.55x KEGE and ~285 deg through `WeatherField` zones (`wxfield`, SEMANTICS 17) only after calibration on CO092 / CO095 and the Dowd Junction RAWS (DJTC2), plus a +-40 % wind dimension in the ensemble. Effect: F3. Risk: a slower nominal is anti-safe; ship with R1. Measure: `p16`, `p17`, `p24`, `p25`.

**R7 Resolution policy.** Treat the 200 m corridor tile as advisory or rebuild it from the 90 m tiles (`tools/fetch_sim_tiles.py`); make the ember window a metric disc (now `ceil(r/cell)`: 270 m vs 200 m). Effect: removes the 5x scatter. Measure: `p8`, `p9`.

**R8 Harness arms (one product hook).** (i) Hidden truth: `truth_ros_scale` 1.5 / 2.0 on the nominal member only (default 1.0), so P10 coverage is tested against a faster truth; (ii) CBH >= 2 m as the slow-truth control (`p15`); (iii) union acres for pair / late (they report sim-f1 only: Vail pair 14,537 vs union 19,481; Vail's late fire is a dud, 248 acres); (iv) flags for stalls (<= 20 acres) and tile-edge reach (Vail canonical extreme hits 15.7 km by hour 9); (v) the 13:13 arm (R2).
