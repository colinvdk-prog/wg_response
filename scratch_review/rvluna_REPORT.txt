## (a) Concrete bugs and errors, severity-ranked

### 1. CRITICAL — corridor safety treats missing forecast coverage as safe

[LegOracle skips every sample whose raster lookup is outside the grid](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/safety.py:353). If all samples are outside, `latest_safe_depart_s` remains infinity and the route is accepted without any fire information. This is deliberately pinned by [test_corridor_outside_raster_is_safe](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/tests/test_safety.py:235), which places a lethal raster in another region and expects the unmodeled route to pass.

Minimal repro already present in the suite:

- Arrival raster: uniformly lethal at `NOW`, centered near 40°N, 100°W.
- Route: 34.1°N, 117.1°W to 34.1°N, 117.05°W.
- Result: `safe=True`, `worst_margin_h=inf`.

There is no dispatch-side invariant that every road polyline and safe zone stays inside every incident raster. A route that exits a clipped fire domain therefore becomes progressively less constrained precisely where the model has no data. This contradicts the broader “absence is not clearance” safety posture.

### 2. HIGH — weather changes are not integrated across an edge; burned cells are never reconsidered

A cell is processed once using the weather bucket at its arrival time, at [spread.py:617](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:617). The full edge then uses that single rate at [spread.py:641](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:641), even if traversal spans later weather buckets. The cell is never reactivated when wind changes.

Arithmetic demonstration for a 90 m edge:

- ROS is 0.01 m/s for the first hour, then 1.0 m/s.
- Current code, source ignited at `T0`: `90 / 0.01 = 9,000 s`, arrival at 2.5 h.
- Piecewise propagation: 36 m burns during hour one; remaining 54 m burns in 54 s, arrival at 3,654 s.
- Error: 5,346 s late on one edge.

At flank/backing rates or before a wind event, a traversal can span many hours. The claim that hourly non-FIFO effects are negligible is therefore unjustified. This is dangerous-polarity error: late P10, late alerts, and later crew deadlines.

### 3. HIGH — heterogeneous edge cost ignores the destination cell’s ROS

The solver computes parameters only for source cell `idx` at [spread.py:618](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:618), then applies that source rate over the entire center-to-center edge at [spread.py:640](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:640). The project’s own MTT specification requires an A/B rate average at [spread_model.md:269](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/docs/research/spread_model.md:269).

For a 90 m interface with directional ROS `R_A=0.1 m/s` and `R_B=1.0 m/s`:

- Current A→B cost: 900 s.
- Current B→A cost: 90 s.
- Documented arithmetic-mean cost: `90 / 0.55 = 163.6 s` in either direction.
- Half-cell serial integration would give 495 s, also symmetric.

Thus the implemented travel metric depends on which side of a fuel boundary ignites first. Fuel mosaics can gain almost a full slow-cell or fast-cell traversal at each boundary. None of the three legacy fingerprint scenarios samples a heterogeneous burnable interface.

### 4. HIGH — the 73.5% “coverage” and 0% early-miss claims are one-sided by construction

Hindcast scoring defines early miss as `p90 < T0` at [hindcast_lib.py:640](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/tools/hindcast_lib.py:640). All perimeter seeds are assigned `T0` at [spread.py:482](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:482), and every traversal cost is nonnegative. Therefore every exterior modeled arrival satisfies `p90 ≥ p10 ≥ T0`; `p90 < T0` is impossible.

Consequently, the “possible coverage” condition at [hindcast_lib.py:645](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/tools/hindcast_lib.py:645),

`p10 ≤ T1 and p90 ≥ T0`,

collapses algebraically to only `p10 ≤ T1`. The committed artifact confirms this identity: `cover` and `p10_nec` are exactly equal at [gust_sweep.json:24](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/scratch_review/gust_sweep.json:24).

Interpretation:

- 26.5% is a valid lower bound on definite late misses.
- 73.5% is an upper bound on cells whose P10 might not be late; the true burn could have occurred anywhere in `(T0,T1]`.
- 0% early miss supplies no evidence against overprediction.
- P90 is effectively unvalidated by the headline statistic.

The PIT histogram and head/area ratios contain some overprediction information, but the headline table does not.

### 5. MEDIUM — spread combines equivalent wind speeds instead of Rothermel φ vectors

The spread path derives a slope-equivalent wind at [spread.py:579](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:579), vector-adds it to midflame wind, then feeds the magnitude back through the wind equation. The correct helper instead combines `φ_w` and `φ_s` at [rothermel.py:381](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/rothermel.py:381).

Because `φ_w=C·U^B` is nonlinear, vector-adding speeds is not equivalent to vector-adding spread factors. The source itself acknowledges approximately +8% aligned and −10% opposing-slope errors. The latter is dangerous-polarity late bias. This should be treated as a physics deviation, not merely an integrator convention.

### 6. MEDIUM — ensemble count and perturbation magnitude contradict the contract/handoff

The contract defines default `ensemble_n=6` as “perturbed members (+1 nominal)” at [contracts.py:954](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/contracts.py:954). The implementation treats six as the total count at [spread.py:790](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:790), producing one nominal plus five perturbed members.

Wind and moisture perturbations also receive an unconditional `×0.5` at [spread.py:803](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:803). With five stratified draws, the extrema are `u=±0.8`; default wind variation is therefore only:

`±0.8 × 0.25 × 0.5 = ±10%`,

not the handoff’s ±25% at maximum lead. Direction reaches only ±24°, not ±30°, because its five strata also stop at ±0.8.

This may be the calibrated historical behavior, but it is not the declared configuration semantic and can materially mislead safety-band tuning.

### 7. MEDIUM — safety-critical configuration validation is incomplete

`validate_config` checks only five conditions at [contracts.py:1116](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/contracts.py:1116). It accepts, among others:

- `circuity=0`, which divides by zero at [safety.py:198](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/safety.py:198);
- negative `route_margin_min`, weakening the corridor law;
- negative erosion/sample radii;
- `wind_gust_blend < 0` or `> 1`;
- nonpositive horizon or road speed.

The live CLI accepts any float for `--gust-blend`, and simulator configuration can apply arbitrary `ResponseConfig` fields before this incomplete validator runs. Invalid safety configuration can therefore fail open, suppress wind, or crash.

### 8. LOW — the numerical ROS floor can spread through an extinguished fuel cell

The traversal floor is `1e-4 m/s` at [spread.py:91](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:91) and is applied after directional ROS at [spread.py:641](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/spread.py:641). A burnable cell whose Rothermel reaction intensity is exactly zero therefore still propagates.

At the permitted 30 m native resolution:

`30 m / 0.0001 m/s = 300,000 s = 83.33 h`,

which is inside the default 96 h horizon. It can produce a one-cell false burned halo through fuel that is at or above moisture of extinction. The polarity is conservative, but it is not physically or semantically “no spread.” Zero ROS should produce infinite traversal cost; the floor should apply only to small positive numerical values.

### 9. LOW — raster access silently wraps negative indices

`Raster.get` directly indexes `data[r*ncols+c]` at [contracts.py:459](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/wgresponse/contracts.py:459), and `set` does the same. Python negative indices wrap rather than fail. For a 2×2 raster, `get(-1,0)` returns the first value of the last row instead of rejecting the coordinate.

Current geo/spread callers normally bounds-check first, so this is an API boundary hazard rather than a demonstrated operational failure.

### 10. LOW — reported “median” head ratio is the upper middle observation

For an even number of scored windows, pooling selects `sorted_values[len//2]` at [hindcast_lib.py:693](C:/Users/colin/wg_response/tools/hindcast_lib.py:693). With 14 windows this is the eighth observation, not the conventional mean of observations seven and eight. The published 1.7724× is therefore an upper-middle order statistic, not a standard median.

## (c) Top three recommended fixes

1. Replace the MTT edge kernel.

   Use both source and destination directional ROS, integrate traversal across weather boundaries, and use an event-expanded/FIFO-safe formulation that allows an already-burning cell to accelerate when weather changes. Correct the φ-vector wind/slope combination in the same change. Add independent two-cell fixtures for slow→fast fuel, fast→slow fuel, wind increase during traversal, wind decrease, and opposing wind/slope.

2. Make safety rasters fail closed at their boundaries.

   Require every corridor sample to fall inside every required incident raster. If not, reject the route as `forecast_domain_incomplete`, expand the terrain/arrival domain to include the entire road polyline and safe zone, then recompute. `None` rasters must be unavailable/error states, never “everywhere safe.” Extend `validate_config` to enforce finite nonnegative margins/radii/horizons and strictly positive travel denominators.

3. Rebuild the validation gates around independent quantities.

   Replace the headline “coverage” label with “fraction not definitely late by T1,” report that it is an upper bound on P10 adequacy, and remove 0% early miss as evidence. Score overprediction with head/area ratios and shorter intra-day perimeter windows. Add exact reference outputs from BehavePlus/pyretechnics for several FBFM40 fuels and mixed-fuel/time-varying MTT fixtures. Rebaseline byte hashes only after these independent expectations pass.

## (d) Overall fitness judgment

**Shadow alerting now: conditionally fit, grade C.** Internal shadow/dry-notify operation can continue because it does not expose clients or crews to automated decisions and can accumulate the missing forecast evidence. Outputs must be labeled experimental; 73.5% must be described as an upper bound on P10 adequacy, not calibrated interval coverage. Shadow telemetry should explicitly count off-domain clients/routes and weather-transition edge exposure.

**Client alerting after only the existing ledger blockers: not fit, grade D+.** Closing gel, persistence, smoke, and live ember-radius blockers would still leave two dangerous late-arrival errors in the core spread solver and an inadequately validated P10 band. Client notification timing would remain vulnerable at weather changes and fuel boundaries.

**Crew dispatch/routing: not fit.** The off-raster fail-open rule is an independent life-safety blocker. No operational crew route should be approved until complete raster coverage is enforced and the corrected spread kernel has been re-hindcasted.

A controlled client-notification pilot becomes defensible only after the three fixes above, fresh green execution of the full suite, independent physics fixtures, and a new out-of-sample hindcast showing the corrected P10 late-bound performance.
